## About Me
For over eight years, I’ve been devoted to crafting intelligent systems that bridge human creativity and machine precision. My work spans software engineering, embedded development, data science, and technical leadership—always with a focus on bringing ideas into tangible form.  

My journey into AI began 15 years ago while building robots at university. Facing the limits of traditional control systems, I realized that what machines lacked was not motion, but *understanding*. That realization has guided my path ever since.

At Sony, I engineer subject-recognition AI that allows photographers to focus on expression rather than mechanics. As a freelance consultant, I help organizations integrate intelligence into their products—designing and implementing systems such as:
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** frameworks for adaptive planning  
- **Content recommendation engines** powered by embedding-based preference modeling  
- **Real-time conversational systems** combining LLMs with domain-specific retrieval layers  

My approach is grounded in **an agile mindset of adaptability and craftsmanship**—iterating quickly, learning continuously, and delivering value through refinement. I find rhythm in the process of building: observing, testing, and distilling complexity into simplicity.

Looking ahead, I aim to create technology that **amplifies human intelligence and creativity**, helping people see, think, and make in ways that once felt beyond reach.  
Beyond engineering, I explore how human-centered organizational philosophies—like **Holacracy** and **Teal organizations**—can unlock collective creativity within teams.

[On this website](../blog), I write primarily in Japanese about natural language processing, machine learning, and software engineering.
