## About Me

For more than nine years, I’ve been building AI-powered products that bridge human creativity and machine intelligence. My work spans embedded AI, computer vision, generative AI, recommendation systems, data science, and technical leadership—always with a focus on turning research and ideas into reliable products.

My journey into AI began 15 years ago while building robots at university. Facing the limits of traditional control systems, I realized that what machines lacked was not motion, but _understanding_. That realization has guided my path ever since.

At Sony, I’m a Lead Software Engineer in embedded AI, leading a development team that builds subject-recognition and tracking features for mirrorless cameras. Our work includes people tracking and AI-powered autofocus, helping photographers concentrate on expression and timing instead of focus mechanics.

I’m a hands-on technical lead rather than a full-time manager. I work directly on the hardest engineering problems while setting technical direction, reviewing architecture and design, and making the final calls on quality, performance, and resource trade-offs. I also help break down work, support engineers’ decisions, and create systems that reduce cognitive load and rework across the team. My responsibility is not only to write strong code, but to enable the team to deliver the right product with dependable quality.

Until February 2026, I also worked as an independent AI consultant and research engineer, partnering with organizations from product strategy and prototyping through production. My work included:

- **LLM applications, RAG, GraphRAG, and AI agents**
- **Language, speech, and real-time conversational AI**
- **Recommendation systems** powered by embedding-based preference modeling
- **Local LLM and inference environment evaluation**

My approach is grounded in **adaptability and craftsmanship**: prototype quickly, learn from real users, and refine systems until advanced technology becomes dependable and useful. I enjoy working across the stack—from algorithms and low-level optimization to product design and technical direction.

Looking ahead, I want to create technology that **amplifies human intelligence and creativity**, helping people see, think, and make in ways that once felt beyond reach. I’m also interested in how autonomous, human-centered organizations can unlock the creativity of teams.

[On this website](../blog), I write primarily in Japanese about natural language processing, machine learning, and software engineering.
